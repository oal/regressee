## Overall impression and workflow diagnosis

The application is visually calm and unusually careful about evidence integrity.
Its central weakness is **task hierarchy and spatial ownership, not decoration**.
Choose project → establish website/access → choose pages/sizes → capture → explicitly
save baseline → check fixed inventory → inspect/classify → separately select updates
→ confirm consequences → retain/share history is the correct product story. The UI
has these capabilities but often presents them as adjacent settings and ledgers,
not one understandable sequence with a dominant next safe action.

Three distinctions are essential, not removable complexity: a visual difference is
not a defect; an intentional review decision is not a baseline update; captured
screenshots are not automatically the saved reference. Current equal-weight control
groups make those distinctions expensive to learn. Repeated caution is extraneous
load; learning the consequences of a deliberate update is useful germane load.

### What's working — preserve these three foundations

1. **Evidence honesty.** Originals are untinted; Difference, Overlay and Heatmap
   are labeled analysis, not brand color. Capture-only views use one original.
   Failed/missing screenshots are not unchanged and are not eligible updates.
   [Compare][review-wide], [Difference][difference], [capture gaps][capture-gaps].
2. **Deliberate consequences and local ownership.** Project context, explicit saves,
   baseline confirmation, older versions and inspection-only history distinguish
   safe browsing from mutation. ZIPs exclude sessions/cookies; ignored areas do not
   redact original screenshots. [Projects][projects], [historical check][recorded],
   [export][export]. Preserve draft/stale-identity guards and advanced capabilities.
3. **Honest waiting, rather than decorative reassurance.** Task surfaces communicate
   actual processed/saved/failed/remaining work, unknown-duration activity without
   invented percentages, and what cancellation preserves. Gap Details names causes
   and next steps. [Capture progress][progress], [gap Details][gap-details],
   [setup download][download]. Waiting can use spare space; inspection cannot.
