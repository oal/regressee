What a check does

You point Regressee at your site once and save a baseline. After that, every check revisits the same recorded pages and screen sizes. Nothing is rediscovered, skipped or silently dropped.

  1. Revisit

    Every page in the saved baseline list is opened at the same screen sizes, with the same capture settings. A check never discovers new pages on its own.

  2. Compare

    Screenshots are compared against the baseline, and DOM snapshots are diffed line by line. Failed or missing captures are reported as not compared, never as unchanged.

    ChangedNo significant changeNot compared

  3. Review

    Before, after and difference views sit side by side, with overlay, heatmap or a draggable split. Adjust sensitivity or mark ignored areas afterwards; the original captures are never altered.

  4. Approve

    Select the changes you accept and save a new baseline version. Earlier versions and every run stay in History until you delete them.

What’s in the box

Real app pixels from a demo project. Set your appearance to System, Light or Dark and the screenshots follow, exactly as the app does.

Guided capture, not a test script

Start from a URL. Regressee walks the visible links inside limits you set in plain language: website boundaries, skipped paths, a page limit. It then shows the page list it found. Capture full pages or just the visible screen, at mobile, tablet and desktop sizes, as screenshots, DOM snapshots or both.

  • Page-list preview before anything is saved
  • Time and disk estimates on the capture plan
  • Its own private browser, nothing for you to install

Screenshots and structure, judged separately

A page can look identical and still change underneath. Screenshot and DOM results are reported and approved independently, and the DOM view shows an inert, normalized diff of the live document, including template contents and open shadow roots.

  • Added and removed lines without executing the page
  • Balanced sensitivity, adjustable without recapturing
  • Areas you ignore never alter the original screenshots

Baselines you approve, history you keep

Nothing becomes a baseline until you save it. Approve selected results into a new version, keep whole-baseline saves explicit, and inspect every run, version and storage figure in History. Cleanup previews exactly what it would remove.

  • Earlier versions remain until you delete them
  • Storage use, low-space warnings and manual cleanup
  • Results, notes and decisions recorded per run

Also in the box

Sites with a login
Sign in through the app’s managed browser. Saved sessions are encrypted with an OS-protected key, and production and staging logins stay separate.
Share a baseline
Export a baseline ZIP and import it as a new local project. Login sessions and secret keys never travel with it; recipients use their own.
CLI and agent skill
The same engine runs in a terminal, with a portable skill for Claude Code, Codex or GitHub Copilot. Reports are local HTML files.
Local by default
Projects, screenshots and baselines live on your machine. No account, no cloud, nothing uploaded; diagnostics are previewed before you save them.

Good to know

What Regressee will and will not tell you.

A difference is not a defect
Regressee shows what changed; you decide what it means. Failed or missing captures are never shown as unchanged pages.
Coverage has limits
Fonts, operating systems, dynamic content and slow-loading pages can all affect comparisons. Every capture records warnings; review them before trusting a result.
DOM snapshots are not redaction
Script bodies, inline handlers and form values are excluded, but hidden text and attributes can still reveal private content. Inspect exports before you share them.
Screen sizes are emulated
Phone and tablet profiles are Chromium emulation, not real iOS or Safari devices, and full-page capture has size limits.
Visit only sites you are allowed to check
Use read-only test accounts where you can. Page loads and scripts can change server data even when you never click or submit anything.
